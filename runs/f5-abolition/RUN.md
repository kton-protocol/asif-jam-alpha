# f5-abolition

**From:** fog
**Started:** 2026-09-18

## What I am trying

Price the phrase "on track to disappear". Extend the balanced-panel trend line to zero.

## What happened

Fog crosses zero in **2043**. It is a straight line through 35 points, extended.

Then the same arithmetic on `gew`, same stations, same days: thunderstorms cross zero in **2065**.
Nothing in the data distinguishes the two extrapolations. If the first is a finding, so is the
second, and the second is absurd.

Also fitted a constant-proportional model (log-linear, -3.00%/yr) to the same 35 points. On the
original scale R2 = 0.688 against the linear model's 0.728 — four points of variance, which is not
a basis for preferring either. It reaches 1.94% in 2043 and **never reaches zero at all**.

**Published,** and this is the one where we are closest to the line. The figure is real, the
crossing year is real, and the sentence "fog will be gone by 2043" is a sentence about the model,
not about Austria. `out/abolition.txt` and `out/abolition-both.png` carry the thunderstorm line
next to it, so the reductio ships with the rhetoric.
