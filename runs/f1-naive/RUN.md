## What I am trying

`examples/fog/starter.R` unmodified, to see the figure the claim is supposed to rest on.

## What happened

7.13% (1990-1994) -> 4.95% (2020-2024), i.e. **-30.6%**: "roughly a third", exactly as the TASK
promised. It would have made a perfectly good headline.

**Abandoned as a headline.** f0-audit shows its denominator falls 34% over the same window, so a
third of the visible decline could be arithmetic. We could not tell how much, and neither could
anyone re-running it. Also the script bins 2025 into a "2025-2029" period from one part-year, and
that bar goes back *up* to 6.61% — a bar we would have had to explain or crop.

**Published anyway**, because it is the figure we started from and it is the figure the other side
would find if we did not show it.
