## What I am trying

The worst thing available. Every station-day is a row; treat a missing `nebel` as zero fog and
divide by all of them. The denominator becomes exactly constant, which sounds like a fix.

## What happened

7.09% -> 3.25%, **-54.2%**, against -30.6% on reported days only. The recode buys 23.6 extra
percentage points of headline decline, entirely by counting stations 20, 35 and 170 as fog-free
every day after they stopped looking.

**Rejected.** A day on which nobody looked is not a day without fog.

**Published** so that the rejection is in the record rather than in our memory of ourselves. If
we had wanted a bigger number this is where it was, and we did not take it.
