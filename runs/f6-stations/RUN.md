# f6-stations

**From:** fog
**Started:** 2026-09-18

## What I am trying

Find out whether "fog in Austria" is a country or two weather stations.

## What happened

It is closer to a country than we feared, and we would have published this even if it were not.

All six panel stations decline; slope negative in all six, p<0.05 in four. But the decline is
lopsided: station 131 (15.55% -> 4.05%) and station 30 (7.94% -> 1.94%) together account for
**65.7%** of the panel's lost fog days. Station 131 starts at nearly three times the panel's
quietest station and ends indistinguishable from it — a site-level change that large is as
consistent with something changing *at the site* as with regional climate, and this file has no
metadata to tell us which.

Leave-one-out is the reason we still stand behind f3. Dropping any single station:
slope stays between -0.1081 and -0.1984 (full panel -0.1704), p never worse than 3.6e-07, and the
period change ranges -43.8% to -56.7%. Drop station 131, our biggest contributor, and it is still
-43.8% at p = 3.6e-07.

**Published.** The worst single-station-deletion result, -43.8%, is still comfortably more than
the "roughly a third" we claim.
