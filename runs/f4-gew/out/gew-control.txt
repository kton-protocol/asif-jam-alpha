WHAT THIS TEST CAN AND CANNOT DO -- read before quoting it.
It shows year-to-year thunderstorm variation does not explain year-to-year fog variation.
It CANNOT separate a common secular decline, because a covariate that trends with year is
collinear with year and the fit hands the trend to whichever term is in the model. This
regression could not have come out any other way. It is reported because we ran it, not
because it settles anything.

Ratio nebel:gew, first five years 0.814, last five years 0.751 (-7.8%).
If gew is taken as a pure observing-effort proxy, that ratio is what is left of the fog
decline, and it is small. We do not think gew is a pure effort proxy. Neither reading is
decidable from this file.
